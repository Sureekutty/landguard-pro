Step 4: Critical Pre-Purchase Checkpoints & Field Diligence Checklist
Buyer Mandatory Rules
1. Physical Field Stone Demarcation:
Seller must arrange the Firka Surveyor to plant concrete corner boundary stones matching FMB triangulation measurements before advance payment.
2. Patta Sub-Division Verification:
Confirm sub-division is officially carved out in Taluk office e-Services registry, not an undocumented private family partition.
3. Hand Over Original 30-Year Parent Deeds:
Must deliver Original Partition Deed, Settlement Deed, and Original Mortgage Discharge Receipt before registry.
4. Zero-Arrears Panchayat Kist Receipts:
Produce official Village Panchayat tax receipts up to date proving no local body tax or agricultural kist arrears.
5. Consenting Signatures of Family Heirs:
Spouse and all adult children of registered owner must sign as consenting witnesses in the Sale Deed to prevent future partition claims.
6. Same-Day SRO Token EC Verification:
Conduct an online EC search on the morning of registration at SRO to confirm no same-day second sale or attachment was filed.
